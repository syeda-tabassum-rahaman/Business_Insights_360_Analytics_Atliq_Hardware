# AtliQ Hardware: Business Insights 360

## 📌 Project Overview
AtliQ Hardware is a rapidly growing global hardware company that sells PCs, printers, mice, and computers to customers across the globe. However, to compete with industry giants and make data-informed decisions, they needed an enterprise-level dashboard to track their KPIs across all major departments. 

This project provides a comprehensive 360-degree view of the business, enabling stakeholders to analyze historical data, compare it against targets, and uncover actionable insights to drive profitability and operational efficiency.

## 🎯 Key Departmental Views
This dashboard is divided into five core operational views to serve different stakeholders:

* **Executive View:** High-level overview of key metrics (Net Sales, Gross Margin, Net Profit, Market Share) with YoY performance and top customer/product breakdowns.
* **Finance View:** Detailed P&L (Profit & Loss) statement analysis to understand the financial health and profitability across markets.
* **Sales View:** Customer performance and unit economics analysis to identify top-performing clients and regions.
* **Marketing View:** Product performance tracking, evaluating gross margins, and identifying segments that require targeted marketing campaigns.
* **Supply Chain View:** Forecast accuracy analysis to identify out-of-stock and excess inventory risks, helping optimize supply chain operations.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Skills Used
* **Tool:** Power BI (Power Query, DAX, Data Modeling)
* **Data Sources:** MySQL Database, Excel/CSV files
* **Data Modeling:** Snowflake Schema with Fact and Dimension tables
* **Techniques:** Advanced DAX measures, customized tooltips, dynamic filtering, bookmarks, and parameters.

## 💡 Key Business Metrics Tracked
* **Net Sales & Gross Margin %**
* **Net Profit %**
* **Forecast Accuracy % & Net Error**
* **YTG (Year to Go) & YTD (Year to Date) Analysis**
